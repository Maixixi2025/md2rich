Frequently Asked Questions

Basics

Is md2rich really free?

Yes, 100% free with no signup. The site is supported by unobtrusive ads.

Does my content get uploaded to a server?

No. md2rich runs entirely in your browser. Your text never leaves your device.

Which platforms does md2rich support?

X Articles, LinkedIn, Medium, Substack, Ghost, WordPress, and any editor that accepts rich text paste. For LinkedIn we recommend pairing md2rich with the 'Markdown Here' browser extension.

Platform-Specific

Why doesn't LinkedIn work as well as Medium?

LinkedIn's rich text paste support is unreliable. We output Markdown as plain text with a tip — use a browser extension like 'Markdown Here' to render formatting inside LinkedIn's editor.

Does X Articles support really work?

X Articles is an X Premium feature. Our whitelist is based on the open-source mdpress spec — the actual X Articles composer behavior in 2024+ may differ. If you have X Premium and find issues, please open an issue.

Does md2rich support tables?

Tables render in the preview. For LinkedIn and X Articles, table tags get stripped on paste — use bullet lists as a workaround. For Medium and plain HTML export, tables work fully.

Privacy & Security

Is my data tracked?

No analytics, no cookies, no tracking pixels, no fingerprinting. md2rich is 100% client-side.

Can I use md2rich offline?

After the first load, the page is cached by your browser. You can use it offline for the duration of your browser cache.
